Hospital Document Workflow

How Patient Reports Move Inside SIT Students Hospital

Designed specifically for our hospital stakeholders—connecting our central diagnostic laboratory, specialist OPD doctors, and patients under one tamper-proof hospital registry.

STEP 1 • CENTRAL LAB

Lab Technician Uploads Report

Our hospital lab staff uploads the patient's blood test, X-ray, or spirometry report. The system checks its SHA-256 hash to prevent accidental duplicate uploads.

STEP 2 • SECURE STORAGE

Encrypted Archiving & Indexing

The file is encrypted using AES-256-CBC on the hospital server, while OCR indexes clinical parameters so doctors can search by condition or keyword.

STEP 3 • MULTI-DOCTOR ACCESS

Shared Across Hospital Doctors

Whether a patient visits General Medicine, Orthopedics, or Pulmonology inside SIT Students Hospital, each attending doctor sees the same chronological patient timeline.

STEP 4 • PATIENT & QR CHECK

Patient Access & Instant Verification

Students and patients can log in to download their signed PDF report with an embedded QR code or generate a temporary link for family consultation.
