Our Mission & System Architecture

Why We Digitized SIT Students Hospital Records

In a busy campus hospital, students and patients often consult general physicians, orthopedic specialists, and pulmonologists across different visits.

01 • UNIFIED FILE

Single-Hospital Record Sharing

All departments inside SIT Students Hospital share one patient UHID timeline so doctors can review previous blood tests or X-rays before prescribing new ones.

02 • RECEPTION BILL ID

Passwordless Patient Access

Patients don't need to remember complex accounts. The Reception Desk issues a unique Bill Number (`BILL-2026-XXXX`) that lets patients view and download their reports.

03 • LAB PRIVACY

Strict Role Separation

Lab technicians only see ordered tests and upload new diagnostic PDFs, while doctors select individual patients to write checkup prescriptions and clinical notes.

04 • TAMPER-PROOF

AES-256 & SHA-256 Integrity

Every diagnostic report is encrypted off-chain with AES-256-CBC and anchored with a SHA-256 hash on our internal hospital ledger to prevent duplicates and tampering.
