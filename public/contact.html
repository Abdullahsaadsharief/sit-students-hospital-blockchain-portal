Step-by-Step Patient Guide

How Patients & Students Use the Hospital Portal

Follow these 3 simple steps when visiting SIT Students Hospital for a checkup or diagnostic lab test.

STEP 1 • RECEPTION COUNTER

Collect Your OPD Bill Receipt

At the Reception Desk (/reception), the receptionist registers your details, assigns your consulting doctor, and gives you an official OPD Bill PDF with a unique Bill Number (e.g., BILL-2026-1001).

STEP 2 • DOCTOR CHECKUP & LAB

Consultation & Lab Upload

Your doctor selects your patient file, records your checkup vitals & medicine prescription, and orders any needed lab test. The Central Pathology Lab uploads and anchors your PDF report.

STEP 3 • SECURE PATIENT ACCESS

View Reports With Bill No. + Access Code

Open the Hospital Portal or Reception Page, enter your Bill Number + 8-Character One-Time Access Code (issued on your OPD slip), set your personal password, and download your Doctor Prescription PDF and Verified Lab Reports.
